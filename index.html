CAPTURE WORKFLOW

How to connect multiple streams

01For each stream, add its player as a source in a separate OBS instance or separate OBS endpoint.
02Give every OBS connection its own WebSocket port and Replay Buffer. Keep each stream on its matching agent card.
03Set that agent’s clip criteria and priority phrases, then connect it. The app saves matching chat or speech phrases for review.

All connected agents can capture at once. Formatting runs in a background queue. Stream Clipper captures OBS scenes you control; it does not fetch video directly from a platform URL.
